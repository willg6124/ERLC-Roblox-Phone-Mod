Liberty County
Phone Mod

A dual-interface desktop phone overlay for Emergency Response: Liberty County private servers — live 911 dispatch, GPS map, contacts synced from your server, messaging, and an owner-only Admin app.

  1. Server Owner runs the Host (ERLC-Host.exe) and enters the ER:LC Server Key there only.
  2. The Host shows a 6-digit pairing code. Players enter it here — the key itself never touches a player's PC.
  3. Pick iOS or Samsung One UI, choose your duty, and your phone goes live.
